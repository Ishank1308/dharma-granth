## What the queries say

1. **Verse look-ups dominate.** Most queries are a Gita verse in Devanagari, Romanised Hindi or by number, often with
   "in hindi", "meaning in english" or "full shlok": `दैवी ह्येषा गुणमयी मम माया दुरत्यया` (19 impressions),
   `mamevansho jiv loke meaning in hindi` (19), `chaturvarnyam maya srishtam in hindi` (25),
   `sarva dharman parityajya sloka meaning in hindi` (10), `11.15 in hindi` (7), `3.15 in hindi` (7).
2. **They rank but do not get clicked.** Most sit at positions 8 to 11 with 0 clicks: the bottom of page one.
   The snippet and title have to win the click there.
3. **Brand is weak.** `dharm granth` (22 impressions) averages position 7; `dharma granth` 1.9; spellings vary
   (`dharmgranth`, `dharam granth`, `धर्म ग्रंथ`).
4. **Concept questions.** `karma is universal law meaning in hindi` (126 impressions, position 12.5, 0 clicks),
   `samsara meaning in hindi` (26), `ahimsa meaning in hindi and english`, `yajna meaning in hindi`.
5. **Rare texts get clicks.** Ravana Samhita (8 clicks, position 6.2), Narada and Matsya Purana chapters, Atharvaveda
   chapters, Prithvi Sukta (position 1–2.7): the long tail where few other sources exist.
6. **Number-only queries** (`17.26/2`, `11.29*12`, `4/37`) look like verse-number look-ups from other contexts and
   rank poorly; not a priority.

## Implications for the plan

- Verse pages are the main search surface. Make sure each answers the query in the first screen (the verse, the
  Hindi meaning, the English meaning) and that the title and description match the query forms above.
- The Gita verses people search for have Hindi and English meanings already; the work is snippets and internal
  links, not new pages.
- Add `WebSite` structured data with `alternateName` spellings of the brand, and check the Organization entity.
- Concept pages (karma, samsara, ahimsa, yajna) have demand at position 9–12: strengthen those existing pages.
