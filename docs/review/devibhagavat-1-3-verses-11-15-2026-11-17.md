# Devī Bhāgavata Skandha 1.3 — verses 11–15, source-reading release

Primary numbered Sanskrit witness: https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_purana/devIbhAgavatam01.html (Skandha 1, Adhyāya 3, verses 11–15); consulted 17 November 2026.

## Verse correspondence
- 3:11 `एकोनविंशत्साहस्रं` — Garuḍa 19,000; Kūrma 17,000; the Sanskrit names Hari and Kūrma as traditional speakers.
- 3:12 `एकाशीतिसहस्राणि` — Skanda 81,000; Sūta closes the naming and counting of the Purāṇas.
- 3:13 `तथैवोपपुराणानि` — transition to Upapurāṇas; Sanatkumāra, then Narasiṃha.
- 3:14 `नारदीयं शिवं चैव` — Nāradiya, Śiva, Daurvāsasa, Kāpila, Mānava, Auśanasa names.
- 3:15 `वारुणं कालिकाख्यं च` — Vāruṇa, Kālikā, Sāmba, Nandi-related, Saura, Āditya; `pārāśara-prokta` has potential attachment ambiguity, so the Hindi does not overcommit it to a title.
