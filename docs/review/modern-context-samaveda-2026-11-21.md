# Modern-context notes: Samaveda (review note, 21 November 2026)

This note goes with the pull requests that add modern-context notes (`lifeLesson`) to the Samaveda. It records how the notes were checked, the caveat each note carries, the sensitive verses, and the facts a reviewer should check first. The notes are AI drafts and have not had a human editorial review.

## Status

- **Coverage:** all 1,886 Samaveda entries now have a modern-context note. 20 had one before this work (1:1 to 1:3, 1:9 to 1:12, 2:1, 2:2, 3:1, 3:2, 4:1 to 4:3, 4:9 to 4:12, 4:660, 4:661); these were not changed. The other 1,866 notes were written on 21 November 2026.
- **Pull requests:** the first 228 went in with #413, then #416 (228), #427 (608, with Vinaya Patrika), #439 (304, with Vinaya Patrika) and the last 498 (4:1378 to 4:1875) with this note.
- **Source file:** `data/hi-commentary/samaveda-rest.json` holds only the `lifeLesson` field. The existing व्याख्या (`explanation`) is unchanged for every entry; this was verified after each publish against master.
- **Size cap:** `MAX_TOTAL_MB` in `scripts/convert-commentary-to-json.ts` was raised from 62 to 70 for Samaveda and Vinaya Patrika (in #413). After this book the published commentary is 68,847 KB of the 71,680 KB cap.

## How the notes were made and checked

- **Writers:** the book was written by several writers in parallel on separate verse ranges (Ishank's choice on 21 November, to finish faster), six runs of 6 to 9 writers. Each writer screened its planned facts against every earlier note in every book and recorded them in a shared claims list, so that two writers working at the same time could not take the same fact. Every batch was then checked centrally before publishing, by one publisher.
- **Register:** every batch passed `scripts/check-hindi-register.mjs` with 0 sentences to fix; no nukta letters; no first person; no तुम-forms; 160 to 185 words per note.
- **Grammar:** each note reads "… यह मन्त्र …" once; no link starts with का, की or के.
- **Facts used once:** a stricter check (rare words or numbers shared with any other note in any book) was run on each batch and over the whole book. Writers ruled out several hundred candidate facts at screening. Pairs that the check still flagged were read one by one; all were judged to be different facts (for example 1:505 YBC 7289 and 1:528 Plimpton 322; 1:530 Rhind papyrus and 4:892 Ebers papyrus; 4:687 reef-sound study and Brahma Sutra 2.2.2.11; 4:710 Arati Saha and Durga Saptashati 5:12 Matthew Webb; 4:1410 Banni buffalo and 4:1721 virda wells). One cross-book repeat was found and fixed before publishing (Vinaya Patrika 46:4, which repeated the harsingar fact of 2:608).
- **Anchoring:** each note says in one sentence what the mantra itself says, following the printed meaning and the existing व्याख्या. Where the printed meaning is unclear the link opens with "मुद्रित अर्थ के अनुसार".

## Standing caveats and the notes that carry them

The Samaveda is a book of Vedic mantras arranged for singing: the purvarchika (single mantras to Agni, Indra, Soma and others) and the uttararchika (mostly threes, so neighbouring mantras often say nearly the same thing). Each note that needs it carries one of these standing sentences. 619 notes needed none.

## Sensitive verses (read first)

- **Apratiratha imprecations, 4:1860 to 4:1871:** war language against an enemy army. Every note carries YG, most also SH; each link turns the verse to inner resolve, panic, aggression or a fault in oneself, and none names any group of people.
- **Dark or black covering read as darkness:** 1:323 and 1:380 (the "black" opposing force, read as darkness and sloth), 1:491 and 4:892 (the black covering, read as the darkness of night).
- **Dog verses:** 1:545, 1:553 and 4:697 follow the printed English "drive away" and read the dog as greed or the ungiving tendency; 4:774 reads it as अदानी वृत्ति. No note uses a word for a person.
- **Brahmin and Aryan or Dasa wording:** 4:668 and 4:826 take ब्रह्माणः / ब्रह्मा as the singers; 4:855 and 4:1867 to 4:1868 read the enemy or the would-be enslaver as a tendency.
- **Other verses flagged in the व्याख्या work:** 1:340 (follows the printed meaning, DN), 1:409 and 4:1005 (the cows following Indra's self-rule, read as shared rules), 1:557.
